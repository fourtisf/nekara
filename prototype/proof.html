The method

Three measurements. No exceptions.

The same formula runs on every call — the ones that worked and the ones that didn't. That's what makes the hit rate mean anything.

Fourteen hard vetoes first

Chain, liquidity floor, age window, cap ceiling, liquidity-to-cap ratio, sell pressure, no vertical entries, real socials, dust flow, wash pattern, fading bid — and the base has to be a bet, so a tokenised equity is refused first. Any single failure kills the signal, however good the rest looks.

Method

How a call is measured

One formula, applied the same way to every call. Nothing is removed after the fact.

Entry
Market cap at the exact second the call fires. Recorded once, never revised.
Peak ×
Highest market cap since entry, divided by entry. A ceiling, not a result — nobody sold there.
Now ×
Current market cap divided by entry, always shown beside peak so the drawdown is visible.
Win
Peak × reached 2.00 or higher. Marked at that moment and it stays marked.
Miss
Settled below 2.00. Counted in every rate on the page.
Dead
Market cap fell under 10% of entry. A win can go dead later — both marks stay.
Hit rate
Wins over all calls in the window. Misses and dead calls stay in the denominator.
Median peak
The middle peak ×, not the average — one moonshot can't carry the number.
Live / Settled
Live for 24 hours from the call, then settled. Settled figures stop moving.
Time to 2×
Only recorded for calls watched live. Never back-filled from historical candles.
